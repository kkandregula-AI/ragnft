RAG
  • No retraining needed
  • Knowledge updated live
  • Cites its sources
  • Lower cost to deploy
  • Great for dynamic data
🧬
Fine-Tuning
  • Bakes knowledge in weights
  • Better tone & style control
  • No retrieval latency
  • Expensive to retrain
  • Great for static expertise

👤 SAMPLE USER QUERY
"What medications are used to treat high blood pressure?"
A patient asks an AI health assistant. The AI has no memory of their records — but it has a Medical FAQ knowledge base indexed and ready.
❌ WITHOUT RAG
"Common treatments include lisinopril and amlodipine…"
⚠ May be outdated · No source · Hallucination risk
✅ WITH RAG
"According to medical_faq.pdf (p.7): ACE inhibitors such as lisinopril…"
✅ Cited · Grounded · Current
📍 HOW THIS QUERY FLOWS THROUGH EACH TAB
1
🏗️ RAG Architecture
See the full system the query will travel through — documents → chunks → vectors → retriever → LLM
2
✂️ Chunking
The Medical FAQ is split into overlapping chunks. "ACE inhibitors for hypertension…" becomes Chunk 34
3
🔢 Embeddings
Our query → [-0.023, 0.411, 0.188…] 1536-dim vector that captures its meaning
4
🔍 Vector Search
Query vector vs all chunk vectors. Cosine similarity: D1=0.984 ✅, D2 (pasta)=0.490 ❌ excluded
5
⚡ Full Pipeline
Top-4 chunks injected into the LLM prompt → grounded, cited answer generated in ~850ms
6
📊 Evaluation
Did it work? Faithfulness=1.0 · Relevancy=0.89 · Context Precision=0.50 — RAGAS grades the answer

📚 Citations & Free Resources
1
Lewis et al. (2020) — "Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks" — Meta AI FREE
The original RAG paper that introduced the architecture. Essential reading for understanding the foundation.
🔗 arxiv.org/abs/2005.11401
2
LangChain Documentation — RAG Tutorials FREE
Practical step-by-step tutorials for building RAG pipelines with document loaders, splitters, vector stores, and chains.
🔗 python.langchain.com/docs/tutorials/rag
3
Anthropic — "Contextual Retrieval" Blog Post FREE
Anthropic's research on significantly improving RAG retrieval accuracy using contextual chunk enrichment.
🔗 anthropic.com/news/contextual-retrieval
4
Gao et al. (2023) — "Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Large Language Models: A Survey" FREE
Comprehensive survey of RAG architectures — Naive RAG, Advanced RAG, Modular RAG. Must-read for practitioners.
🔗 arxiv.org/abs/2312.10997
5
Hugging Face — Fine-Tuning Course FREE
Practical NLP fine-tuning with Transformers, LoRA, QLoRA and PEFT. Python notebooks included.
🔗 huggingface.co/learn/nlp-course
6
Hu et al. (2021) — "LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models" FREE
The landmark paper for parameter-efficient fine-tuning. Explains why you don't need to update all model weights.
🔗 arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685
7
RAGAS — RAG Evaluation Framework FREE / Open Source
The leading open-source framework to evaluate RAG pipelines: faithfulness, answer relevancy, context precision and recall.
🔗 docs.ragas.io
8
Pinecone — "What is a Vector Database?" FREE
Clear guide to vector embeddings, cosine similarity, and how vector databases like Pinecone, FAISS, and Chroma work.
🔗 pinecone.io/learn/vector-database
9
DeepLearning.AI — "Building and Evaluating Advanced RAG" (Short Course) FREE
Free short course by Andrew Ng's team covering sentence-window retrieval, auto-merging, and TruLens evaluation.
🔗 deeplearning.ai — Advanced RAG Course
10
OpenAI — Fine-Tuning Guide FREE
Official guide to fine-tuning GPT models including data preparation, JSONL format, hyperparameters, and evaluation.
🔗 platform.openai.com/docs/guides/fine-tuning
